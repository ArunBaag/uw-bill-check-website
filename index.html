    Your household bills planner

    Pop in what you pay each month (yearly bills like car insurance: divide by 12). Nothing here is saved or sent.

    1. List every bill and its end date. Put a reminder in your phone 4-6 weeks before each contract ends.
    2. Never auto-renew without checking. Out-of-contract broadband, mobile and insurance prices are often the highest.
    3. Spread energy costs evenly. A steady monthly Direct Debit means no winter shock - check it twice a year against your usage.
    4. Pay for the phone, not twice. When a handset contract ends, move to SIM-only.
    5. Fewer bills, fewer dates. Putting services together on one bill makes them easier to keep track of.
    6. Build a small "bills buffer". Even £10-£20 a month set aside helps with surprises like a boiler repair.

    Help you may be entitled to

    Warm Home Discount

    £150 off your electricity bill this winter (October-March) if you get certain benefits - most people get it automatically. Check on GOV.UK

    Priority Services Register

    Free extra support from your energy and water companies if you're older, disabled, unwell or have young children - for example advance warning of power cuts. Ask your supplier.

    Social broadband & mobile tariffs

    Cheaper broadband and mobile deals for people on Universal Credit and some other benefits. See Ofcom's list

    Struggling with energy debt?

    Talk to your supplier first - they must offer affordable repayment plans. Free, independent advice: Citizens Advice.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The bill check and your personal comparison cost nothing, and there's no obligation to switch.

    Do I need all my bills?

    No - fill in what you know. Monthly prices are enough to start; unit rates and contract dates make the comparison more accurate.

    What happens to my details?

    They're saved only in your own browser until you send them, then go only to me. I use them only to prepare your comparison and contact you about it. I delete them 30 days after your switch is completed, or 30 days after you sent them if you decide not to go ahead. See the privacy notice.

    I'm still in a contract - is there any point?

    Yes. I can check your end dates and exit fees and tell you the best time to move - sometimes it's right away, sometimes it's worth waiting.

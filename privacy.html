Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who I am

Your UW Partner is an Authorised Utility Warehouse (UW) Partner and runs this personal website. I am the data controller for the details you send through it. Contact: email.

This is not the official Utility Warehouse website. If you later sign up with UW, UW's own privacy policy applies to what you give UW.

What I collect

  • Your name, postcode, phone number and/or email, and how you'd like to be contacted.
  • The bill details you choose to enter: suppliers, tariffs, prices, contract and renewal dates, exit fees, the number of SIMs, pets and cars, and any notes.

Please don't enter bank details, passwords or account numbers - I never need them on this site.

Why I use it

Only to prepare your personal comparison and to contact you about it (for example about your contract end or renewal dates). The lawful basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending. I don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Where it goes

  • While you fill in the form, your answers are saved only in your own browser (so you can come back later). Use Clear my saved answers at the bottom of the main page to remove them.
  • When you press Send, your details are emailed to me through Web3Forms (a form-to-email service acting on my behalf). If you choose WhatsApp or email instead, they go through that service.
  • I keep them in my own records on my computer to prepare your comparison.
  • This website has no cookies, adverts or tracking.

How long I keep it

Up to 12 months after we last spoke, unless you become a customer (then as needed to look after your account), or sooner if you ask me to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, at any time by contacting me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
